## 6. Known gaps

- **FAQ authoring provenance.** 163 attributes' FAQs were written by Sonnet subagents from the validator, schema, docs and runtime modules, then machine-checked (kinds, counts, orphans, question form, length) and scrubbed of authoring vocabulary; 25 (encryption, decryption, source inputs, `flow_step_id`, `transformation_sql`) were written in-session from the same sources. Spot-reads found them grounded; a full editorial pass has not been done.
- **The Spec Builder does not show FAQs.** The inspector renders `attribute_knowledge.json`; `attribute_faqs.json` is documentation-only until the app grows a FAQ panel (a `Builder.jsx` change plus `npm run build`).
- **Mermaid renders from a CDN.** Material loads Mermaid.js at view time; inside the Databricks App the diagrams depend on that fetch being allowed. Every diagram is accompanied by prose and tables, so nothing is lost if it is blocked.
- Subagent-authored pages (pillars, FAQs) were structurally validated and spot-read, not line-edited; a human read for tone is still worthwhile.
